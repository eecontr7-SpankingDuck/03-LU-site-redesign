Meet the Owner

About Fran

Francine Miller has always believed that flowers can change the feeling of a room and make an ordinary day feel a little more special.

In 1995, she opened Fran's Flowers in San Francisco with a simple goal: create beautiful arrangements while building a flower shop that feels welcoming to everyone who walks through the door.

Fran's Story

How It Started

Fran's interest in flowers started long before the shop opened. She grew up enjoying gardens, arranging flowers for family gatherings, and learning how different colors and textures could completely change an arrangement.

After years of creating bouquets for friends and family, Fran decided to turn that interest into something bigger and opened Fran's Flowers in the Mission District.

Still Growing

Today, Fran's Flowers continues to focus on fresh flowers, thoughtful service, and strong ties to the local community.

Customers visit the shop for birthdays, anniversaries, celebrations, thank-you gifts, and sometimes simply because someone deserves flowers.
